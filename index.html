HOW TO PLAY

Goal: find the 3 red escape keys hidden around the school, then reach the green EXIT door at the west end of the main hallway.
Move: WASD / arrow keys. Hold Shift to sprint (uses stamina).
Flashlight: aim with the mouse, toggle with F. Batteries drain it. Darkness hides keys, items and the ghost.
Doors: gold door keys open locked brown doors.
Secrets: cracked walls hide secret rooms. Press E beside one.
Ghost: it never stops hunting you. Each hit costs health; lose it all and you lose a life.
Pickups: pink hearts heal, green batteries recharge. P/Esc pauses. Touch: drag to move.
